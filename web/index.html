Open. Install. Give it a tilt.

Open the download and drag Hinge into Applications. Allow Screen Recording, turn Hinge on, and set your comfortable open position. It remembers for next time.

This is a development build. If macOS blocks it, approve Hinge in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Requires a supported lid sensor.

A peek behind the bend.

Hinge reads your lid angle 120 times a second. Metal gives your live desktop perspective and blur, with smoothing between readings. Tilt slowly, it follows slowly. Move faster, it keeps up.

Your screen stays on your Mac. No saved recordings. No uploads.
